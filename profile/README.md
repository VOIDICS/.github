## 👋 Welcome

**VOIDICS** is a research group at [CICECO – Aveiro Institute of Materials](https://ciceco.ua.pt), the [Department of Chemistry of the University of Aveiro](https://www.ua.pt/en/dqua), and the [Portuguese NMR Center](https://www.ua.pt/en/cpnmr). As the name suggests, we focus on studying what happens inside the *voids* of porous materials.

Our work aims to understand at the atom-level **why and how** materials work, not just observing *that* they work. To do that we combine advanced experimental methods with atomistic simulations.

## 🔬 Research Areas

- **Porous materials**: zeolites, zeotypes and other inorganic porous frameworks
- **Structural characterization**: solid-state NMR, X-ray diffraction and vibrational spectroscopy
- **Atomistic simulation**: ab initio methods and machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs)
- **Data-driven materials discovery**: reproducible workflows and structured data
